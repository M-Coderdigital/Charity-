About Us

ABOUT UNI YOUTH CHARITY FOUNDATION

UNI Youth Charity Foundation is a youth-driven charitable organization established to serve humanity, build hope, and create positive change within communities. Founded from the spirit and values of Thanksgiving Charity, the foundation is dedicated to supporting vulnerable individuals, promoting compassion, and inspiring people to contribute to the well-being of others.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to bring hope to those in need through charitable activities, community outreach programs, donations, volunteer initiatives, and youth empowerment projects. We believe that every person deserves care, support, and an opportunity to thrive. Through our work, we seek to raise awareness that many members of society require assistance and that everyone can play a role in creating a better future.

UNI Youth Charity Foundation is committed to empowering young people to become impact makers and responsible citizens. We encourage youth to embrace positive mindsets, develop leadership skills, engage in community service, and use their talents and knowledge to solve real challenges within their communities. We firmly believe that young people can achieve great things when they are inspired, united, and guided by a clear purpose.

OUR FOUNDATION

The foundation was established by Brian Munisi, a student pursuing a Diploma in Public Administration, together with his close crew of dedicated individuals serving on the foundation's board. Their shared vision is to inspire a culture of giving, compassion, volunteerism, and community development among young people and society at large.

UNI Youth Charity Foundation also values collaboration and partnerships. The foundation works in partnership with members of the Student Management Council of Kange Health College, including student leaders who are pursuing a Diploma in Clinical Medicine. This collaboration strengthens our ability to promote community health awareness, youth engagement, and social responsibility while expanding the impact of our charitable activities.

OUR VISION

Our vision is to build a generation of compassionate, responsible, and empowered young people who actively contribute to the development of their communities. Through unity, service, and determination, we strive to become a beacon of hope and a catalyst for positive transformation.
